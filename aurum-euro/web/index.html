A public repository of European accounts in Aurum Euro

Each figure is kept as published by the statistical authority. A second figure is computed in Aurum Euro from the fixed metallic definition and the gold price of the same period.

One hundred Aurum Euro equal one troy ounce of gold of fineness 999.9. The original observation multiplied by one hundred and divided by the aligned gold price yields the figure in Aurum Euro.
