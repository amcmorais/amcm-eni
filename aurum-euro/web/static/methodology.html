Method

The Aurum Euro is a metallic unit of account. One hundred Aurum Euro equal one troy ounce of gold of fineness 999.9, after the Wiener Philharmoniker coin of one ounce.

An original observation expressed in euro is not replaced. A derived observation is obtained by multiplying the original figure by one hundred and dividing by the euro price of one troy ounce of gold for the same reference period.

Flows use the average gold price of the period. Stocks use the gold price at the close of the period. Daily market series use the gold price of that day.

Percentages, physical quantities and index numbers are left unchanged. The calculation does not assert whether an economy has expanded or contracted in any welfare sense.
