Methodology

Canonical definition (immutable): 100 €Au = 1 troy oz Au 999.9.

Exchange (time-varying): EUR market price of one troy ounce of fine gold.

Transform: X€Au,t = XEUR,t × 100 / PAu/EUR,t.

Alignment: daily = date; monthly/quarterly/annual flows = period average; stocks = period-end.

The Deflator Thesis is not an input to this engine.
