Sources and the limits of lawful republication

Eurostat

European statistical observations in this repository are taken from the official Eurostat dissemination interfaces (SDMX and the Statistics API). Eurostat’s reuse policy permits republication of those observations with acknowledgement of the authority. Original values are stored unchanged. Derived Aurum Euro figures are the work of this repository and are labelled as such.

STOXX and publicly listed European equities

STOXX Ltd. distinguishes data that any visitor may read on its website from data reserved to licensed customers.

What a visitor may see, not copy into a public repository. Unregistered visitors may consult a short recent history of index values on the administrator’s site (the published file family h_3mxxxxx, three months). Registered users may see one year. The full history (h_xxxxx), daily composition, weights, free-float, component prices and historical membership are restricted. The conditions of use forbid downloading, storing, printing, copying or redistributing that data, and forbid the creation of a derived index, benchmark or historical database from it, except under a licence.

What this repository therefore does not publish. It does not load STOXX Europe 600 or EURO STOXX 50 official files. Attempts to retrieve those files without credentials return a refusal from the administrator. No scrape is substituted. No Wikipedia table, vendor feed or ETF tracking product is treated as a STOXX official series.

What may be stated without a data licence. The existence of the indices, their names, the identity of the administrator, and a summary of the published access rules. Factsheets and methodology papers remain on the administrator’s site. Constituent lists and historical prices of the listed companies, if they are to appear here, must come from a licensed STOXX feed or from another authority that expressly permits republication—not from the restricted files.

Equities as companies. A company’s own regulated disclosures (accounts, prospectuses, stock-exchange filings) are a separate legal source from the STOXX index files. Those filings may be cited where the issuer or the market operator permits. They are not a substitute for STOXX weights or historical membership.

Until a customer licence is in force, the equity wing of this repository remains a reserved place in the schema, not a published series.
