Sources

The repository fetches official series on a schedule, converts monetary euro observations to Aurum Euro, and publishes the result. A person does not import each row by hand.

Eurostat

National accounts and related collections are taken from the official Eurostat SDMX interfaces. Original euro observations are preserved. The published figure is €Au.

European listed issuers

Issuer reports are taken from ESEF packages on filings.xbrl.org (issuers on EU regulated markets) and from each State’s Officially Appointed Mechanism. Line items in euro are converted to €Au. Official last prices of the venues are not published until a republication right exists.

Long scale

Magnitudes in this text follow the long scale, in which the name of an X-llion denotes a million taken as a factor as many times as the prefix indicates: million = 106; milliard = 109; billion = 1012 = M2; trillion = 1018 = M3. The short-scale assignment of “billion” to 109 is not used. The distinction is definitional, not ornamental: billion euros and milliard ounces name different powers, and the table is to be read only under that rule.
