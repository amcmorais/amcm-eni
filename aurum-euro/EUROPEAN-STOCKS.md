# European stocks

A register of companies admitted to the **primary regulated market** of each euro-area State.

This is not a STOXX product. It does not use STOXX files, names, membership, weights or review calendars.

## Sources

1. Identity — national company register and GLEIF (LEI).
2. Admission — the official list of the primary market (MIC in `data/euro_area_venues.json`).
3. Accounts — the Officially Appointed Mechanism of that State; ESEF annual reports; later ESAP.
4. Market prints — official closing price of that venue, only after a written republication right.

## What is published now

The twenty primary venues and their official list and OAM addresses. Issuer rows and prints wait on machine-readable official files or a venue term.

## What will never be published under this title

EURO STOXX 50, STOXX Europe 600, or any series described as equivalent to them.
